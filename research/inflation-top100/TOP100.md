## Why each stock benefits from US inflation

### 1. PRIO — PRIO3 (B3, Brazil) · score 87.9

- **Why inflation helps:** Almost pure Brent-linked oil exporter with USD revenue, a large BRL cost base and a lifting cost of only ~$12.5/bbl, giving very high operating leverage to higher nominal oil and a weaker dollar. Output steps up from 106 kbpd (2025) to ~187 kbpd (2026) and ~204 kbpd (2027) with Peregrino and Wahoo, so EPS roughly quadruples from 2025 even at flat oil.
- **Key risks:** Brent volatility, Wahoo/Peregrino execution and IBAMA licensing, Brazilian export-levy/tax changes.
- **Price:** 59.15 BRL (2026-08-05) · mkt cap ~$9.4bn · consensus EPS 2026: 9.71, 2027: 9.51, 2028: 10.70
- **Data confidence:** medium · Reports in USD; EPS converted to BRL (MarketScreener consensus net income / ~860m shares). MarketScreener 2026 P/E 5.9x matches. Analyst count approximate.
