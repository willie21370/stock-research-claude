## Why each stock benefits from US inflation

### 1. K92 Mining — KNT (TSX (OTC: KNTNF), PNG/Canada) · score 79.3

- **Why inflation helps:** Sells unhedged USD gold (plus copper) against a local PNG cost base that rises more slowly than gold, so an inflation-driven gold rally goes almost straight to margin. The Stage 3 plant roughly triples throughput, so ounces and price per ounce rise together.
- **Key risks:** Ramp-up execution worries (Q2-26 13% EPS miss) and PNG sovereign/security risk.
- **Price:** 16.84 USD (2026-10-01) · mkt cap ~$4.1bn · consensus EPS 2026: 1.50, 2027: 2.36, 2028: 3.00
- **Data confidence:** medium · Price is the US OTC line (KNTNF) so it matches USD EPS. FY2/FY3 from Simply Wall St net income / ~242m shares (Mar-26, pre-selloff). Scotiabank FY27 EPS $2.48. BMO target C$40, RBC C$45.
- **Sources:** [1](https://robinhood.com/us/en/stocks/KNTNF), [2](https://simplywall.st/stocks/ca/materials/tsx-knt/k92-mining-shares/future), [3](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/fy2027-earnings-forecast-for-k92-mining-issued-by-scotiabank-2026-07-17/), [4](https://k92mining.com/news/k92-mining-announces-strong-q2-2026-financial-resu-15387/)

### 2. Vista Energy — VIST (NYSE, Argentina) · score 79.2

- **Why inflation helps:** Sells crude at Brent-linked, USD export-parity prices while much of its cost base is in Argentine pesos, so hot US inflation that lifts USD oil prices drops straight to margins. Production is growing ~30%+ a year (Q2-26 output +32% y/y, revenue +102% after the La Amarga Chica deal), so price and volume compound, and its long-life shale reserves are a hard asset.
- **Key risks:** Argentine political, FX and capital-control risk; pipeline/export bottlenecks out of Vaca Muerta.
- **Price:** 73.32 USD (2026-10-02) · mkt cap ~$7.9bn · consensus EPS 2026: 8.64, 2027: 9.50, 2028: 9.79
- **Data confidence:** medium · FY0 = 2025 adjusted EPS $3.30 (reported $7.0 included a one-off acquisition gain). Revenue consensus not found. 100% of analysts rate Buy; avg target ~$90.
- **Sources:** [1](https://tickflow.io/stock/VIST/forecast), [2](https://vcpscanner.com/valuation/vist/estimates), [3](https://www.frenzycap.com/quote/VIST), [4](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001762506/000119312526187099/d936340d20f.htm)

### 3. Eldorado Gold — EGO (NYSE / TSX, Canada) · score 76.9

- **Why inflation helps:** Mostly unhedged USD gold revenue, and the Skouries mine starting in 2026 adds copper, another inflation-linked real asset. Gold output is guided to grow ~40% over three years, so higher metal prices land on rising volumes for strong operating leverage to a weaker real dollar.
- **Key risks:** Skouries ramp-up delays/cost overruns; Greek and Turkish permitting risk.
- **Price:** 43.0 USD (2026-09-01) · mkt cap ~$8.7bn · consensus EPS 2026: 4.67, 2027: 5.33, 2028: 5.03
- **Data confidence:** medium · Price from 1 Sep 2026; a 18-Sep market cap of $10.5bn would imply ~$51 and P/Es ~20% higher. FY3 from Simply Wall St. Analyst count approximate.
- **Sources:** [1](https://www.eldoradogold.com/investors/news-releases/eldorado-gold-provides-2026-guidance-three-year-outlook-targets-40-gold), [2](https://ca.marketscreener.com/quote/stock/ELDORADO-GOLD-CORPORATION-50060889/finances/), [3](https://www.vcpscanner.com/valuation/ego/estimates)

### 4. Aris Mining — ARIS (TSX / NYSE American, Colombia/Canada) · score 75.8

- **Why inflation helps:** Almost pure, unhedged gold producer selling in USD with costs mostly in Colombian pesos, so an inflation-driven gold rally plus a weaker dollar widens margins sharply. The Segovia expansion and Marmato bulk-mining project lift output through 2027-28, so volume and price both drive earnings.
- **Key risks:** Patchy execution (3 earnings misses in 4 quarters); Colombian security, artisanal-mining and permitting risk.
- **Price:** 19.16 USD (2026-10-01) · mkt cap ~$3.9bn · consensus EPS 2026: 2.41, 2027: 3.60, 2028: 3.69
- **Data confidence:** low · FY0 revenue not found (FY1->FY3 revenue CAGR ~21%). One source shows much lower EPS that looks stale.

### 5. Ero Copper — ERO (NYSE / TSX, Brazil/Canada) · score 75.4

- **Key risks:** BRL strength, Brazilian tax/permitting, and a history of back-loaded production guidance.
- **Price:** 35.07 USD (2026-09-10) · mkt cap ~$3.6bn · consensus EPS 2026: 4.19, 2027: 4.53, 2028: 4.36

### 6. International Petroleum Corp — IPCO (TSX / Stockholm, Canada) · score 74.7

- **Why inflation helps:** A pure USD-priced oil producer whose big Blackrod heavy-oil project is now built (first oil May 2026, 30,000 bopd plateau by late 2027), so capex is largely sunk and higher oil prices fall almost entirely to free cash flow and buybacks. Rising replacement costs for oil-sands projects also raise the value of an asset that is already built.
- **Key risks:** Blackrod ramp-up execution, WCS heavy-oil differentials and a large Q2-26 EPS miss; expensive on 2026 earnings.
- **Price:** 28.99 CAD (2026-10-01) · mkt cap ~$2.4bn · consensus EPS 2026: 0.85, 2027: 2.82, 2028: 3.92
- **Data confidence:** low · Consensus in USD converted to CAD at 1.39. FY2/FY3 from Simply Wall St (Feb-26), set before the 2026 oil spike, so likely conservative. FY0 revenue estimated.

### 9. Steel Dynamics — STLD (NASDAQ, US) · score 65.0

- **Why inflation helps:** Low-cost US mini-mill steelmaker with its own scrap supply. In hot US inflation, domestic steel prices (tariff-protected by Section 232) rise faster than its scrap and energy costs; Q3-26 guidance of ~$5.36 EPS vs $2.74 a year ago shows margins already widening.
- **Key risks:** Cyclical steel demand/pricing; aluminum mill ramp execution.
- **Price:** 242.06 USD (2026-09-04) · mkt cap ~$36.4bn · consensus EPS 2026: 17.16, 2027: 19.69, 2028: 17.45
- **Data confidence:** medium · Not under-the-radar (large cap). 2027-28 revenue from a model, not consensus.

### 10. Taseko Mines — TKO (TSX / NYSE American (TGB), Canada) · score 63.6

- **Why inflation helps:** Its Florence Copper mine in Arizona produces copper cathode inside the US, so it captures US tariff-driven COMEX premiums and reshoring demand on top of the copper price. Florence ramps from 30-35 Mlb (2026) toward ~85 Mlb a year on top of Gibraltar, giving one of the highest earnings leverages to copper of any producer.
- **Key risks:** Florence ramp-up execution and Gibraltar grade variability; expensive on 2026 earnings and only ~3 analysts.
- **Price:** 11.56 CAD (2026-09-18) · mkt cap ~$2.7bn · consensus EPS 2026: 0.41, 2027: 0.69, 2028: 0.98
- **Data confidence:** low · EPS in CAD; price C$11.56 (US$8.38 on NYSE American). FY2 EPS is a two-broker average (Stifel 0.54, NBF 0.84).
