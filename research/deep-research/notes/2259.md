## Q3. Allied Gold deal and M&A

### Takeaway
The C$5.5bn (US$4.01bn) all-cash takeover of Allied Gold, which would have added the Sadiola, Kurmuk and CDI assets, was terminated on 2026-07-29. Neither side expected the closing conditions to be met. It was replaced by a ~US$295m placement for 9.2% of Allied.

### Cited Findings
- Deal announced 2026-01-26 at C$44/share, a 27% premium to the 30-day VWAP. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/zijin-gold-international-acquire-allied-101358177.html), [Lexpert](https://www.lexpert.ca/big-deals/zijin-gold-to-acquire-allied-gold-corporation-for-c55-billion/393941)
- Terminated 2026-07-29 (the outside date) because there was "no reasonable likelihood" the conditions would be met, citing "broader external factors applicable to cross-border transactions of this scale". Zijin Gold instead subscribes for about 12.8M shares at C$32.55 (about US$295m / C$417m), roughly 9.2% of Allied, closing on or before 10 Aug 2026. — [pulse2](https://pulse2.com/allied-gold-secures-295-million-investment-from-zijin-gold-after-acquisition-agreement-ends/), [e-mj](https://www.e-mj.com/breaking-news/zijin-mining-and-allied-gold-acquisition-terminated/), [Allied Q2 MD&A on SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001993344/000162828026053477/alliedgoldmda2026q2.htm)

### Inferences
