## Q1. Latest results, production and earnings quality

### Takeaway
H1 2026 attributable profit rose 46% to RMB 972m on revenue that was flat (+2.5%) at RMB 7.99bn. Higher gold prices, lower unit costs and three months of Simberi consolidation drove the gain. A non-cash fair-value loss on the convertible bond of about RMB 260m (plus RMB 22m of imputed interest) held back the reported number.

## Q2. Simberi / St Barbara transaction

### Takeaway
Lingbao does not control ASX-listed St Barbara Ltd. It holds 50% plus one share of St Barbara Mining Pty Ltd, the Simberi (PNG) project company, bought for A$370m and completed on 2026-04-02. On 2026-09-10 it agreed to buy the remaining interest for A$410m plus a A$43m capex repayment, with completion expected in the March quarter of 2027.

### Cited Findings
- Lingbao subscribed A$370m for its interest in the New Simberi Gold Project. Completion targeted 1 April 2026, and St Barbara booked a gain of about A$0.5bn. — [ASX/St Barbara](https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20260326/pdf/06xtf2t3xcjzcd.pdf)
- Completion of the 50%+1 subscription in St Barbara Mining Pty Ltd took place on 2 April 2026, with consolidation from that date. — [Webull/HKEX](https://www.webull.ca/news-detail/15469714365563904)
- On 2026-09-10 a binding agreement was signed to sell St Barbara's remaining interest plus the Tabar Islands licences for A$410m cash, a A$43m repayment of construction capital funded April–September 2026, a 2.75% NSR on New Simberi gold/silver and a 1.5% royalty on other Tabar minerals (royalties from 1 July 2027). Completion is expected in the March 2027 quarter, subject to PNG and China approvals. — [Mining Weekly](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/st-barbara-sells-off-new-simberi-stake-for-a453m-plus-royalties-2026-09-10); [FilingReader 2026-09-09](https://filingreader.com/news-wire/hongkong/2026-09-09/lingbao-gold-to-buy-remaining-stake-in-png-mine-for-aud410m)

### Inferences
- Implied 100% value is about A$740m (tranche 1) and A$820m+ (tranche 2), so Lingbao is paying more for the second half. Total consideration is about A$823m, roughly RMB 3.9bn.
