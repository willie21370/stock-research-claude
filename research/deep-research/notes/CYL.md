## 3. Guidance history, growth plan and reserves

### Takeaway
The original three-year guidance (Sep 2024) was FY25 105–120 koz, FY26 140–165 koz and FY27 180–200 koz at AISC A$2,000–2,200. Delivery fell far short: FY26 came in at 104 koz. The company said FY27 guidance and an updated 10-year outlook would follow in late Sep 2026, reflecting "changes to permitting timelines, Reserves/Resources, geological understanding, operational delays and processing capacities". The exact FY27 numbers were not retrievable. On 28 Sep 2026 management talked of reaching 200 koz "within two years", with 2.0 Moz of reserves.
