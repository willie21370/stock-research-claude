## Q3. Guidance, growth projects, urea FID

### Takeaway
Guidance: 2026 capex ~$1bn, falling to ~$700M in 2027 (probably excluding urea). RDA oil is targeted at a 28 kbpd exit in 2026 and a 45 kbpd plateau in 2027. The board approved FID on a $2.7bn, 2.1 Mt/yr urea plant (Fértil Pampa, Bahía Blanca) on 17 Jul 2026, with completion by YE2029. It has RIGI incentives and Tecnimont as EPC contractor (~€1.3bn), and BID Invest/IFC loans of up to $1.5bn are under evaluation. Management expects net leverage to peak at about 2x over the next 2–3 years.
