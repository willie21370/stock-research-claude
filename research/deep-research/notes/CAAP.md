## 4. Argentine concession (AA2000) extension

### Takeaway
On 1 Sep 2026, AA2000 and ORSNA signed a contract readjustment (Acta Acuerdo). It was approved by Decisión Administrativa 27/2026, published in the Boletín Oficial on 28 Sep 2026. The deal extends the concession from 2038 to 13 Feb 2049, with a possible further 7 years to 2056 subject to compliance. It also requires an additional $600M (VAT included) of direct investment at Ezeiza: funding must be proven by 30 Jun 2027 and 75% of works completed by 31 Dec 2031. The reference IRR is 14.3%, below the prior 17% (the snippet was truncated).

### Cited Findings
