Section 1 — Business architecture, unit economics, moat & visibility

Business model. Vista is a pure-play Argentine shale oil producer (~86% oil) operating in Vaca Muerta, Neuquén. Its main blocks are Bajada del Palo Oeste/Este, Aguada Federal, Bajo del Choique, 50% of La Amarga Chica (bought from Petronas in 2025), and non-operated interests of 25.1% in Bandurria Sur and 35% in Bajo del Toro (bought from Equinor, closed Q2-26: $712M = $387M cash + 6.22M ADS at $52.2; 243 net wells; 27,733 net acres) VERIFIED. Crude is sold domestically at export parity and exported, at Brent-linked prices.

Moat classification: cost advantage (resource quality plus factory-mode drilling). Competitive insulation 3/5. Vista is a price-taker: the moat protects margin per barrel, not price.

Moat durability: static to slowly eroding. The main erosion forces are (i) core-inventory exhaustion as the basin moves toward step-out acreage (from ~2030), and (ii) cost inflation in Neuquén services and sand as basin activity booms (2026–28).

Build-vs-buy / copy risk: majors (YPF, Chevron, Shell, PAE, Pluspetrol) already hold adjacent acreage, so replication is not the issue. Competition is for rigs, frac crews and pipeline capacity. Hyperscaler or customer in-house risk is not applicable. M&A optionality: plausible buyers are a major seeking Vaca Muerta scale (Chevron, Shell, a Gulf NOC such as ADNOC/XRG). A 25–35% premium is typical, but the probability is low (<10% in 36 months) given the founder's control and Argentine risk. We assign no floor value to it.

Customer ROI / loss avoidance: not applicable in the usual sense, because the product is a fungible commodity. Customer-ROI defensibility is 3/10 and pricing power is nil. In a downturn, revenue simply follows Brent.

Method. We use a single segment (Argentine upstream). Revenue = production × [0.86 × (Brent − $4) + 0.14 × $22/boe gas]. EBITDA/boe = 0.82 × revenue/boe − $10.5, calibrated to Q1/Q2-26. D&A is $20/boe and the effective book tax rate 38%, both calibrated so Q2-26 adjusted net income reconciles (model ~$273M vs actual $259.6M). Interest is 8% on gross debt. 2026 = H1 actual (revenue $1,844M, EBITDA $1,256M) plus a modelled H2. Anchored years: 2026 (guidance plus actuals) and 2027–28 volumes (company plan: 208 kboe/d in 2028). Extrapolation starts in 2029. Oil prices are a house deck in every year. ASSUMED
