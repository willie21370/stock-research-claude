Executive summary

  1. Cheap on any sensible gold price, because nobody trusts the ramp. At A$5.76 the stock trades at 7.4x base FY27 EPS and 3.1x EV/EBITDA. A reverse DCF shows the price discounts about 125 koz flat forever at spot-like gold. Almost none of the 2.0 Moz-backed growth plan is in the price.

The late-September 2026 reset cited permitting timelines, geology, operational delays and processing capacity. Our base FY27 of 120 koz sits 37% below the original FY27 midpoint.

Base FCF stays positive even in FY27, and the stress case never needs equity. Dilution risk is low, unusual for a growth-stage miner.

Reserves are 2.0 Moz across six deposits, including Trident UG at 524 koz and 4.2 g/t and the Cinnamon maiden 232 koz at 3.0 g/t with a LOM AISC of A$2,357. Six ore sources feeding one 2 Mtpa mill is a real structural edge in WA, if haulage, approvals and grade cooperate.
