Why this advice: Aura is a well-run, low-leverage Latin American gold producer. Its growth plan is credible: about 355k GEO in 2026 rising to a modelled 600k GEO by 2031, led by Era Dorada, which is under construction for 2028. At $76.02, though, the shares already price a flat gold price of about US$4,040/oz through 2031 (reverse DCF), roughly today's spot. The probability-weighted 36-month target is $93.6 (+23.1%), far short of the 100% hurdle. The stress case loses about 60%, so upside/downside is 0.38:1. Below-cap collars (US$2,400 call) cost about US$126m of cash in 2027, and the reported AISC of US$1,985/GEO in Q2-26 was above guidance. The business is good, but the entry price is not; wait for ≤$68.

Executive summary

  1. A real growth producer with a clean balance sheet. H1-26 adjusted EBITDA was US$440.5M (+135% y/y), LTM EBITDA is US$801M, and net debt is only 0.2x. Production should rise from ~355k GEO (2026E) to ~540k (2029E) as Borborema and MSG ramp up and Era Dorada starts in 2028 (FS: 111koz/yr at AISC US$1,178).
  2. The stock already prices spot gold. Reverse DCF: at $76, the market prices ~11% GEO CAGR (355k→600k) and a ~52% terminal operating margin at a flat ~US$4,040/oz gold price. That is close to the ~US$4,140 spot. Valuation is fair, not cheap.
  3. Consensus looks too high on the current gold deck. House 2028E EPS is $7.32 vs consensus $12.70 (−42%) and revenue US$1.84bn vs US$2.34bn (−21%). The gap comes from the gold deck (house US$4,000 vs an implied ~US$4,800–5,000), collar cash costs and tax. EPS estimates come from 7 analysts with a wide range (2026: $5.55–10.08).
  4. The hedge book is a hidden liability. ~125koz of collars capped at US$2,400 remain through Jun-2028, worth ~US$219M of intrinsic liability at US$4,150 gold. They cost ~US$126M of cash in 2027 alone and make GAAP EPS swing (Q2-26 +US$126M unrealised gain, Q1-26 −US$24M loss).
  5. Costs are the near-term watch item. Q2-26 AISC was US$1,985/GEO, above the US$1,720–1,865 guidance, driven by MSG at US$5,277. H2 needs ~207k GEO, against 75k in Q2, to hit the guidance midpoint.
  6. The asymmetry is poor at this price. Probability-weighted upside is +23.1% against a stress loss of −60.2%, a skew of 0.38:1 versus the ≥3:1 required.

Verdict: HOLD. Base-case targets: 12-mo $80.5 · 24-mo $94.9 · 36-mo $92.9. Probability-weighted: $75.8 / $91.3 / $93.6.

Section 1 — Business architecture, unit economics, moat & contractual visibility

Aura runs six producing assets: Aranzazu (Cu-Au-Ag, Mexico), Apoena (Brazil), Minosa/San Andrés (Honduras), Almas (Brazil), Borborema (Brazil; first gold 2025) and MSG (Brazil; acquired Dec-2025). It also has two development projects, Era Dorada (Guatemala; under construction, first production H1-2028) and Matupá (Brazil; FS Mar-2025, no construction decision announced). 2026 guidance is 340–390k GEO at AISC US$1,720–1,865 VERIFIED.
