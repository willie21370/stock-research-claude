Section 1 — Business architecture, unit economics, moat & contractual visibility

Business model & moat

Eldorado is a mid-tier producer with gold mines in Türkiye (Kisladag heap leach, Efemcukuru), Canada (Lamaque complex, Quebec) and Greece (Olympias polymetallic), plus two new copper-rich mines: Skouries (Greece, copper-gold porphyry) and McIlvenna Bay (Saskatchewan, copper-zinc-gold-silver VMS, acquired with Foran on 14 Apr 2026). Moat type: cost advantage / efficient scale through permitted, long-life orebodies, not pricing power. Competitive insulation: 2/5. The product is a fungible commodity, so the only moat is position on the cost curve and the scarcity of permitted EU copper.

Moat durability. Widening to 2028 as Skouries' copper by-product credits pull consolidated by-product AISC down. After 2030 it is static to eroding: Kisladag and Efemcukuru deplete and grades fall. The force most likely to erode it is fiscal take in Türkiye and Greece (royalty scales tied to metal prices, corporate tax changes; Q2-26 already saw a Turkish CIT-rate change). Watch the 2027 budget cycles.

Build-vs-buy / copy risk

  • Incumbent replication: a new EU copper-gold porphyry would take 10–15 years to permit and more than $1.3bn of capex (Skouries cost about $1.315bn plus $260M accelerated operating capital). It is effectively not replicable inside the forecast window.
  • Platform/hyperscaler copy: not applicable.
  • Customer in-house build: not applicable. Smelters and refiners cannot substitute for ore.
  • M&A floor: after Skouries is de-risked, EGO is a credible target for a senior producer seeking EU copper and Canadian gold. A typical 25–35% premium would value it at $49–53 on today's price. Türkiye exposure and the recent change of control at Foran lower the odds. Probability of a bid within 36 months: about 10% ASSUMED.

Customer ROI / loss-avoidance

Gold and copper are bought at benchmark prices. There is no customer ROI to defend and no pricing power, but demand is deep and the product cannot become obsolete. A customer budget freeze does not apply; the risk is the macro price of the metal. Customer-ROI defensibility: 5/10. Neutral, because revenue depends on the benchmark, not on customer choice.
