Executive summary

  1. Earnings are real but mostly priced. H1 2026 attributable profit rose 46.45% to RMB 972m on flat revenue of RMB 7.99bn (+2.5%). The drivers were gold price, lower unit costs and three months of Simberi. Adding back a ~RMB 282m non-cash CB loss gives H1 run-rate profit of about RMB 1.25bn. At 12.7x our normalised 2026E EPS, the market already gives credit for most of it. VERIFIED
  2. Simberi is the whole growth thesis. With 100% ownership from the March 2027 quarter, a sulphide expansion aiming for more than 200koz/yr at AISC of 1,100–1,400/oz would take mined gold from ~6.8t (2026E) to ~11.3t (2030E) in our base case. The ramp timing, capex and reserves could not be verified. That is the base case's largest single uncertainty.
  3. Gold beta cuts both ways. A ±10% move in gold moves 2027E EPS by about ±19% (Section 2). The smelting business, which runs on purchased concentrate, adds revenue but dilutes gold leverage, and its volumes are already being cut (bullion output down 3.9t in H1).
  4. The capital base is growing faster than the buyback. Shares rose about 7% in 2026 through the CB conversion at HK$17.83 and a placement at HK$26.16. That is only partly offset by a 30m-share buyback and the controlling shareholder's announced buying. Buying out Simberi (A$453m) leaves a ~RMB 1.8bn funding gap in 2027.
  5. The valuation fails the mandate. The probability-weighted 36-month target is HK$30.0 (+18.3%). The probability of clearing 2x is 10%. Upside to stress-loss skew is 0.27:1 against a 3:1 target.

Section 1 — Business architecture, unit economics, moat & visibility

Model: Lingbao (Henan) runs two businesses. (1) Mining: gold mines in Henan, Inner Mongolia, Xinjiang and Kyrgyzstan, plus Simberi (PNG) since April 2026. H1-26 mined gold was 2.98t; segment revenue RMB 2.64bn; segment profit RMB 1.39bn (+37.1%). (2) Smelting & refining: it buys third-party and own concentrate and sells gold bullion (6.89t in H1-26, down 3.93t YoY), with copper, silver and sulphuric acid as by-products. Most revenue is low-margin pass-through smelting; most profit comes from mining. VERIFIED (H1-26 summaries)
