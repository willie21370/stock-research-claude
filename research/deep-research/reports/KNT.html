Section 1 — Business architecture, unit economics, moat & contractual visibility

K92 owns and operates the Kainantu gold-copper-silver mine (Kora/Judd vein systems, underground) in PNG's Eastern Highlands. The 862 km² tenement also holds the Blue Lake porphyry and Arakompa targets. It sells a gold-copper concentrate under offtake to Trafigura, and 14 drill rigs are active.
