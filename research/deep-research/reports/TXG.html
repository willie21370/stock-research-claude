Section 1 — Business architecture, unit economics, moat & contractual visibility

Model and moat. Torex is a single-complex producer: the Morelos Complex in Guerrero, Mexico, comprising ELG Underground, Media Luna (Cu-Au-Ag skarn), and EPO (first ore late 2026). It sells gold doré and a copper concentrate at market prices. The moat is a cost/efficient-scale type: a built, permitted mill and infrastructure that new entrants in Mexico would struggle to replicate under the current permitting regime. Competitive insulation: 2/5.

Moat durability. Static to slowly eroding. The eroding force is grade decline and depletion at ELG/Media Luna plus Mexican cost inflation (peso, PTU, royalties). Reserve life of about 10–11 years means the "moat" must be re-earned through drilling (ELG added about 2 years of reserve life in 2025) by about 2030.

Build-vs-buy / copy risk. Gold has no platform or hyperscaler risk. An incumbent could not quickly replicate a permitted Guerrero complex; a greenfield equivalent would take 7–10 years and more than $1.5B. As an M&A target, Torex is credible: a net-cash, ~450 koz/yr North American producer. Likely buyers would be mid/senior producers wanting Mexico/copper exposure (e.g., Agnico Eagle, Alamos, Equinox; speculative). A typical 25–35% premium would mean roughly C$87–94. Mexico risk thins the buyer list, so we give the floor only modest weight.

Customer ROI / loss avoidance. This section does not really apply. Buyers are refiners and concentrate traders paying spot less TC/RCs, so there is no pricing power and no budget-freeze risk (gold demand is counter-cyclical). Customer-ROI defensibility: 3/10.

  • Conservative (22%): Gold drifts to $3,800, the peso stays strong and PTU and royalties keep rising. AISC is stuck around $2,100–2,250. EPS halves, and Los Reyes slips but is still built from cash.
  • Base (43%): Gold holds $4,400. Guidance is met, EPO delivers, AISC is about $2,000. EPS is US$6–7 and FCF is used for buybacks. The multiple compresses modestly as the market prices reserve depletion.
  • Thesis (18%): Gold at $5,000–5,200. Los Reyes is approved in 2027, and drilling at Media Luna West and ELG adds years of life. EPS doubles by 2030.
  • Aggressive (7%): Gold at $6,000 (the JPM-type bull case). Torex either receives a bid or re-rates to about 9x, and buybacks shrink the share count about 20%.

Threshold probability (2× = C$139.4): 12-mo 7%; 24-mo 7%; 36-mo 25%. Probability of a >30% drawdown at some point in the window: ≈40% (stress plus conservative = 32%, plus about 8% path risk within the base case given gold volatility).

EV cross-check. PW 36-mo C$84.0 vs DCF midpoint C$94.2: a 11% gap, inside the 25% tolerance. We trust the multiple-based PW more, because the DCF assumes perpetual reserve replacement.

Reflexivity. The valuation does not depend on cheap capital (net cash, self-funded). It depends heavily on sentiment toward gold and generalist flows into miners, which can reverse within weeks. That is a separate failure mode from operating execution, which plays out over quarters.
